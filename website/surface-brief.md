# IntelNet Website Surface

## Scope

Homepage for the IntelNet Browser project. Mode: Persuade.

## Audience and job

The homepage serves both everyday users evaluating an accessibility-focused browser and developers evaluating an open-source architecture. It must help a visitor understand the product within one viewport, experience the page-understanding workflow, then choose a path to GitHub or a future download.

## Direction contract

### THESIS

IntelNet is a browser with an understanding layer. The homepage refuses the category-default arrangement of a generic hero followed by feature cards; it shows the browser doing the work in the opening composition.

### OWN-WORLD

Minimal premium software: an immersive near-black first viewport, a white browser surface as the hero object, soft blue-violet light, precise rules, and generous negative space. The visual language should feel capable and calm, closer to a high-end platform launch than to an accessibility campaign or editorial poster.

### STORY

A visitor sees a page become legible, chooses one of three concrete workflows, reads how the product is assembled, and reaches GitHub. The copy names what IntelNet does and avoids unverified download, customer, or benchmark claims.

### FIRST VIEWPORT

The left half carries a large two-line statement and one primary action. The right half is a dimensional browser window containing a real-feeling article surface and a dark assistant panel speaking a synthetic page summary. The browser is the visual proof; no abstract gradient or decorative illustration competes with it.

### FORM

User-pinned direction: minimal premium software in the spirit of Apple/Vercel, with capability as the first impression. The signature interaction is the tabbed workflow demonstration, where the assistant transcript changes while the page frame stays stable.

### FINISH

unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
